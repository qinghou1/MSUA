Graduate Certificate in Urban Analytics

The Graduate Certificate in Urban Analytics requires the completion of 15 credits. Students follow one of two curriculum options based on their prior academic background.

Requirements overview Option A Option B Completion timeframe

Option A is for students who have a background in Urban and Regional Planning and related programs.

Option B is for students with a background in computer and information science, electronic and computer engineering, or related fields.
