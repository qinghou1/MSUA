MSUA Program Requirements

The Master of Science in Urban Analytics (MSUA) requires the completion of 30 credits. The curriculum includes required core courses - including a 6-credit Master Research Project - and electives in urban and regional planning and machine learning.

Additional Course Policies

Students may count up to two 3000- or 4000-level courses (6 credits maximum) from departments other than URP toward graduate credit. The source recommends the following for students without a computer science background:
